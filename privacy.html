TapBPM Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

TapBPM - Tap Tempo Counter does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

What the app stores

Your settings and any tempos you save are kept only on your device, in the app’s own storage. They are never transmitted anywhere.

What the app does not do

TapBPM - Tap Tempo Counter has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. It makes no network connections. It does not use the microphone, camera, location, contacts, or photos.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Children

TapBPM - Tap Tempo Counter collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
