TapBPM - Tap Tempo Counter Support

Tap tempo and metronome for iPhone.

How do I get an accurate reading?

Tap along for at least eight beats — sixteen or more is better. Watch the strip under the number: when the marks stop jumping around, the tempo has settled. TapBPM fits a line through every tap rather than averaging the last few, so the reading keeps tightening the longer you tap.

The number is half or double what I expected

You probably tapped every other beat, or on every eighth note. Use the ÷2 or ×2 buttons beside “beats per minute”.

How do I start over?

Tap Reset, or just pause for a few seconds — the next tap begins a new reading.

What does the strip under the number mean?

Each mark is one tap, showing how far it landed from a perfectly steady beat: early above the line, late below. Flat marks mean your timing is steady and the reading is trustworthy. A tap that was clearly fumbled is greyed out and left out of the result.

Can I change how it looks?

Tap Theme. You can change the accent colour, the background, the number colour, the font, and light or dark mode. Your choices are remembered.

The metronome stops when I lock my phone

It shouldn’t — the metronome is built to keep playing with the screen locked. If it stops, please get in touch and say which iPhone and iOS version you’re on.
